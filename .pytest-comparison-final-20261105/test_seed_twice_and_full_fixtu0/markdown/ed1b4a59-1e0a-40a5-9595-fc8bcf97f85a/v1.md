Evidence-Grounded Reading Support: A Fictional Pilot Study

This synthetic paper is authored for the Paper Review Lab demo.
Objective: evaluate whether source-linked notes help readers inspect scientific claims.
Method: 24 participants read the same two short texts in a within-subject study.
Participants received either plain summaries or summaries with source locations.
The allocation order was counterbalanced, but participants were not blinded.

Results and Limitations

Participants located supporting passages in a median of 42 seconds with source links,
compared with 67 seconds with plain summaries.
The study reports descriptive statistics only and does not establish causation.
Limitations include a small convenience sample and only two reading tasks.
The authors claim a practical interface contribution, not universal scientific novelty.
